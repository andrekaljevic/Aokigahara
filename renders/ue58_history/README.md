# UE5.8 history frames

Two of the owner's 15 September 2026 renders from the Unreal build, kept to show the step that
`docs/engine_selection/PROJECT_HISTORY.md` describes. Each image is a pair shrunk to 640 × 360 per side.

| File | Left | Right |
|---|---|---|
| `cam09_earliest_vs_v13.jpg` | camera 09 ("ConiferIMG013"), earliest render | the same camera at v13 |
| `cam85_v7_vs_v13.jpg` | camera 85 at v7 | the same camera at v13 |

From v7 to v13, 87–99% of pixels changed on all seven cameras. Every view became 3.7–4.3 stops brighter than the
valued frames g–j, near-black shade disappeared, and a dark, closed forest turned into an open pine park on lawn-like
ground.
